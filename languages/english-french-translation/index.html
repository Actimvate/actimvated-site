FAQ

French translation questions.

Do you translate Canadian French?+

Tell us the document is for Canada and the translator will use Canadian vocabulary and conventions rather than those of France.

Can you handle French documents from African countries?+

Yes. Contracts, reports and official documents from francophone Africa are translated into English with the same two-linguist process.

How much does French translation cost?+

We price per word, depending on the direction, subject area and deadline. Send your files and you will receive a fixed quote within one business day, before any work starts.

Who translates into French?+

A native French speaker translates into French, and a second linguist reviews the work independently before delivery. Translations into English are done by native English linguists.
