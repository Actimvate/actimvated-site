FAQ

Vietnamese translation questions.

Why do diacritics matter in Vietnamese?+

Tone marks change meaning, so missing or wrong diacritics produce real errors. The second linguist checks them in every file.

Can you translate Vietnamese supplier documents into English?+

Yes. Contracts, quality documents and specifications from Vietnamese suppliers are translated into English with the same two-linguist process.

How much does Vietnamese translation cost?+

We price per word, depending on the direction, subject area and deadline. Send your files and you will receive a fixed quote within one business day, before any work starts.

Who translates into Vietnamese?+

A native Vietnamese speaker translates into Vietnamese, and a second linguist reviews the work independently before delivery. Translations into English are done by native English linguists.
