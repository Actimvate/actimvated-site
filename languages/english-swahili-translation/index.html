Kiswahili · EN ⇄ SW

English to Swahili translation, done by native speakers.

Swahili is an official language in Tanzania, Kenya and Uganda, and a working language of the African Union and the East African Community. It is the most widely used language for reaching audiences across East Africa. We translate in both directions, English to Swahili and Swahili to English, with a second linguist reviewing every job.

FAQ

Swahili translation questions.

Do you translate Swahili for both Kenya and Tanzania?+

Yes. Tell us the main audience and the translator will adjust vocabulary; for both, we use a style widely understood across East Africa.

Can you translate Swahili app and website content?+

Yes. Send the files in their original format with any character limits; the reviewer checks consistency across screens.

How much does Swahili translation cost?+

We price per word, depending on the direction, subject area and deadline. Send your files and you will receive a fixed quote within one business day, before any work starts.

Who translates into Swahili?+

A native Swahili speaker translates into Swahili, and a second linguist reviews the work independently before delivery. Translations into English are done by native English linguists.
