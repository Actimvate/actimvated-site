Privacy notice

How we handle your details and files.

Last updated 2026-11-07.

Who we are

Actimvated (translate.actimvated.com) provides translation services. Contact: admin@actimvated.com.

What we collect

When you use the quote form or email us, we receive your name, email, phone number if given, company if given, project details, your message and any files you send.

Why we use it

Only to prepare your quote, carry out and deliver the work you order, invoice you, and reply to you. We do not sell your data or use your documents for any other purpose.

Who sees it

Your files are shared only with the linguists assigned to your job. The quote form is delivered to our inbox by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a form-to-email service; it emails the submission and files to us. We sign client NDAs on request.

How long we keep it

Quote requests that do not go ahead are deleted within 12 months. Project files are kept only as long as needed for the job, any revisions and our legal records. Ask us at any time to delete your files sooner.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you by emailing admin@actimvated.com.

Cookies

This site sets no tracking or advertising cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.
