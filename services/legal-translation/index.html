Service 02 · Legal

Legal translation where every term matters.

Legal documents need precise terms, consistent defined terms and the original clause structure preserved. Each job is translated by a native linguist experienced in legal text and reviewed by a second linguist.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do you provide certified or sworn translations?+

Requirements differ by country and authority. Tell us where the document will be submitted and we will confirm what we can provide before quoting.

Will you sign an NDA?+

Yes. We are happy to sign your NDA before you send any documents.

How is the price calculated?+

Per word, depending on the language pair, subject area and deadline. You receive a fixed quote within one business day, before any work starts.
