FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can you work with software string files?+

Yes. Send the files in their original format with any character limits, and we return them in the same format.

Do you use our glossary?+

Yes. Send any glossary, style guide or earlier translations and they will be followed throughout.

How is the price calculated?+

Per word, depending on the language pair, subject area and deadline. You receive a fixed quote within one business day, before any work starts.
