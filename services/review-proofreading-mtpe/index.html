FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is MT post-editing?+

A native linguist corrects machine-translated text so it is accurate and reads naturally. Tell us the quality level you need and we will quote for it.

Will you tell us what was wrong?+

Yes. Corrections are tracked, and significant issues are flagged so you can judge the original translation.

How is the price calculated?+

Per word, depending on the language pair, subject area and deadline. You receive a fixed quote within one business day, before any work starts.
