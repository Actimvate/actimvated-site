FAQ

Questions, answered.

Which languages do you cover?+

English to and from Arabic, Chinese, Danish, French, Greek, Korean, Latvian, Mongolian, Russian, Shona, Spanish, Swahili, Swedish, Turkish, Uzbek and Vietnamese. If you need another pair, ask: we will tell you honestly whether we can staff it.

Who actually translates my documents?+

A native speaker of the target language with experience in your subject area. A second linguist then reviews the translation before delivery.

How do you price?+

Per word, depending on the language pair, subject area and deadline. You receive a fixed quote within one business day, before any work starts, so there are no surprises.

Can you provide certified or sworn translations?+

Requirements differ by country and authority. Tell us where the document will be used and we will confirm what we can provide before quoting.

How is confidentiality handled?+

Your files are shared only with the linguists working on them. We are happy to sign your NDA before you send anything.

We are an agency. How do we start?+

Email studio@actimvated.com with the pair and a short sample. We will complete a free test and send our rates and capacity.
